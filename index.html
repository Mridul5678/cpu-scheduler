📖 Operating Systems CPU Scheduling Formulas & Concepts

Core Timing Equations

  • Turnaround Time (TAT) = $Completion\,Time - Arrival\,Time$
  • Waiting Time (WT) = $Turnaround\,Time - Burst\,Time$
  • Response Time (RT) = $First\,CPU\,Start\,Time - Arrival\,Time$
  • CPU Utilization = $\frac{\text{Total Time} - \text{Idle Time}}{\text{Total Time}} \times 100\%$
  • Throughput = $\frac{\text{Total Processes Completed}}{\text{Total Time}}$

Novelty & Advanced Metrics

  • Jain's Fairness Index: $$J = \frac{(\sum_{i=1}^n x_i)^2}{n \sum_{i=1}^n x_i^2}$$ Evaluates equity of turnaround penalty ratios $(TAT_i / BT_i)$. Scale: $[1/n, 1.0]$.
  • Highest Response Ratio Next (HRRN): $$R = \frac{W + S}{S} = 1 + \frac{W}{S}$$ Dynamically prioritizes waiting jobs to eliminate indefinite postponement.
  • Optimal Time Quantum Heuristic: $80\%$ of CPU bursts should be shorter than the time slice $q$ to optimize responsiveness.

Jain's Fairness Index

$$J(x_1, x_2, \dots, x_n) = \frac{(\sum_{i=1}^n x_i)^2}{n \sum_{i=1}^n x_i^2}$$ Where $x_i = TAT_i / BT_i$ represents the turnaround penalty of process $i$.

HRRN Response Ratio

$$R = \frac{W + S}{S} = 1 + \frac{W}{S}$$ Prevents starvation by dynamically raising the priority of long-waiting jobs ($W$).

Optimal Time Quantum Heuristic

In Round Robin, the time quantum $q$ should be chosen such that approximately 80% of CPU bursts are shorter than $q$, minimizing context switch overhead while maintaining interactive responsiveness.

SRTF Optimality

Shortest Remaining Time First is provably optimal for minimizing Average Waiting Time ($AWT$) among all scheduling algorithms, but is susceptible to starvation of long processes under continuous arrivals.
